# Trial and licence

## What the app does

- **No key:** everything works. The status line reads *No licence: Work Journal keeps working, free trial keys available*.
- **Trial key:** the status line shows *Trial: N days left*.
- **Licence key:** the status line shows *Work Journal Pro, N days left*.
- **Expired or unreadable key:** the status line says *Licence expired on <date>* or *Licence key not valid: <reason>*, and the app still works.
- No feature is ever disabled, hidden or degraded by the licence state.

The status line appears in the menu-bar menu, at the bottom of the Journal window, in Settings and in the Licence window. The app only verifies a key on your Mac against a built-in public key. It cannot create keys and sends nothing anywhere.
